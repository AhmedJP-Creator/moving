Frequently asked

Got questions?

Do you speak English?
Yes — our entire client-facing team communicates fluently in English. All quotes, planning, and moving day coordination happen in English. No surprises.
How do I get a quote?
Send us photos of your belongings via WhatsApp, LINE, or the form on this page. We'll send back a detailed, no-obligation quote within a few hours — no in-person visit needed.
Do you offer same-day moves?
Yes, subject to availability. For small moves or urgent situations, we can often accommodate same-day requests. Contact us as early as possible to check availability.
Can you handle building move-in/out rules?
Absolutely. We're experienced with Japanese apartment procedures — elevator reservations, floor protection, building manager forms, and time restrictions. We coordinate all of this on your behalf.
What payment methods do you accept?
Cash, bank transfer, PayPal, Wise, Revolut, and PayPay. No Japanese bank account required. We confirm payment terms clearly before your move date.
What areas do you cover?
We currently serve the Kanto region — Tokyo, Kanagawa, Saitama, Chiba, Ibaraki, Tochigi and Gunma. If you're unsure whether your area is covered, just message us and we'll let you know.
Do you supply packing boxes?
Yes — once your move is confirmed, we deliver reusable eco-friendly boxes to your door before moving day. We collect them afterward too. Cheaper and greener than buying new ones.
Is storage available?
Storage is currently out of service while we set up new facilities. We expect to reopen it soon — contact us to be added to the waitlist and we'll notify you when it's available.
